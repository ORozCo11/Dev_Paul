# VMS "What do I do next?" audit, 21 November 2026

Method: every sidebar page was opened as the Admin, Custodian and mechanic of one barangay and read for three things: does the page say what needs doing, is the next action one click away, and does the user learn what happens after they act. The ticket and schedule flows were read in the code and exercised through the API during the QA pass. Nothing below is built unless marked **Done**.

## A. What was done today (Done)

| Page | Change |
|------|--------|
| Readiness Check | Now a to-do list: only vehicles that need a check and can be checked, longest-waiting first. Tick several and confirm them in one go (a blocked vehicle is skipped with its reason). Columns say why it needs a check and who checked last. "Show all vehicles" brings back the rest. |
| Ready to Respond | Shows who checked, when, and how many days the check stays valid (lapses soonest first). A Re-check button appears with 2 days or fewer left. |
| Not Ready to Respond | Shows the reason, what makes the vehicle ready again, the ticket link, and a Propose repair button when it needs repair but has no ticket. |
| Notices | The Custodian is told each morning which readiness checks lapse within a day. |
| Schedule approval | If a proposed schedule has no mechanic, the Admin now picks one in the approval dialog instead of hitting an error. |

## B. Gaps found, ranked

Severity: **High** = the user can get stuck or does the wrong thing; **Medium** = the user has to work it out; **Low** = polish.

### B1. Across every page

| # | Sev | Gap | Proposal |
|---|-----|-----|----------|
| G1 | High | Dashboard items say "View ticket" or "View vehicle" for every task. The user does not learn what they are expected to do (approve, verify, check). | Name the action on the button: "Approve proposal", "Verify repair", "Check vehicle", "Assign mechanic". Same words as the button on the page it opens. |
| G2 | High | A ticket or schedule has no single place that says "it is now X's turn". Status chips alone do not tell the user who must act. (The Next Step column was removed on request, so this goes on the detail page, not in the table.) | A one-line banner at the top of every ticket and schedule detail: "Waiting for the Admin to approve" / "Waiting for Justine to verify the repair", with the person's name and how long it has waited. |
| G3 | Medium | Empty lists show a generic line ("No vehicles here yet, click +"). | Empty states that say what happens next and give the button ("Nothing to verify. New repairs appear here once a mechanic submits one."). |
| G4 | Medium | Notices (bell) exist in dozens of types; the text is informative but it is not certain each one opens the exact page and item. | Make every notice open the item it is about; group by "needs your action" and "for your information". Needs a walk of each notice type. |
| G5 | Medium | Page hints are dismissible and the wording differs per page. | One short "What this page is for / what to do" line per page, re-openable from a "?" icon. |
| G6 | Low | Badge numbers on the sidebar change meaning (unseen count vs waiting count) and drop to zero after a visit. | Show only "needs my action" counts; leave "unseen" to the bell. |

### B2. Ticketing process

| # | Sev | Gap | Proposal |
|---|-----|-----|----------|
| T1 | High | A declined or cancelled proposal leaves the vehicle marked Needs Repair. The Custodian is told in a notice to record a Good check, but nothing on the ticket or vehicle offers that. | On a declined or cancelled ticket and on the vehicle, show "Problem still marked. Record a condition check" with a button that opens the check for that vehicle. |
| T2 | High | The Admin's ticket cards leave out items waiting for verification, so an Admin cannot see that work is stuck at the Custodian. | Show For Verification on the Admin cards and a "waiting N days" tag past 2 days. |
| T3 | Medium | No ticket progress bar. The five stages (proposed, approved, in repair, verification, closed) are not shown. | A small stepper at the top of the ticket detail, current stage highlighted, with the person responsible under it. |
| T4 | Medium | No due date or age on tickets. High priority looks the same on day 1 and day 20. | Show "open for N days" and sort or tag overdue ones by priority (for example High over 3 days). |
| T5 | Medium | Repair type has four names (in-house, external shop, standard repair, cannibalization), and users asked what they mean. | Two choices: In-house (with a "uses parts from a retired vehicle" checkbox) and Outside shop. Needs your decision. |
| T6 | Medium | A rejected repair goes back to the mechanic, but the mechanic's list does not say it was rejected or why until the ticket is opened. | Mark the task "Rejected: fix and resubmit" with the Custodian's reason in the list row. |
| T7 | Low | Return to Admin and No repair needed are two buttons that users confuse. (Colours added; wording still similar.) | One-line explanation under each button, shown on hover and in the dialog. |

### B3. Scheduled services process

| # | Sev | Gap | Proposal |
|---|-----|-----|----------|
| S1 | High | A schedule with no mechanic could never become a job. | **Done** (approval needs a mechanic and offers a picker). |
| S2 | High | A due schedule turns into a job only when the scheduler runs. If the scheduler is not running on the server, nothing happens and nobody is told. | Show "Due today, no job yet" on the schedule row if it is past due without a job, and add the scheduler to the deployment checklist. |
| S3 | Medium | The Custodian proposes a schedule but does not see afterwards whether it was approved, declined or became a job, except by notice. | A status line on each schedule row: "Approved, job starts on Dec 3" or "Declined: reason". |
| S4 | Medium | Recurring schedules plan the next one only after the job is verified; the Custodian is not shown that. | After verification, show "Next service planned for DATE" in the notice and on the vehicle. |
| S5 | Low | The Condition Monitoring page shows "Next scheduled service" but the vehicle profile does not. | Add it to the vehicle profile summary. |

### B4. Readiness and condition

| # | Sev | Gap | Proposal |
|---|-----|-----|----------|
| R1 | Medium | Availability, condition and readiness are three separate words on three pages. New users ask "why is it Available but not Ready?". | A small legend ("Available = not in the shop. Condition = Good or Needs Repair. Ready = the Custodian confirmed it, valid 7 days") on the vehicle list and profile. |
| R2 | Medium | A confirmed check cannot be undone if it was a mistake. | An "Undo" in the notice for a few seconds, as the Archive action already has. |
| R3 | Low | Readiness Check (to-do) and Condition Monitoring both end in "check" and both live under the Custodian. | Keep both, but put one line on each page linking to the other ("Looking for the daily readiness confirmation? Go to Readiness Check"). |

### B5. Other pages

| Page | Sev | Gap | Proposal |
|------|-----|-----|----------|
| Admin Dashboard | Medium | Cards show counts (Pending Approvals 1, Overdue 1) but they are not all clickable to the filtered list. | Every card opens the matching filtered list. |
| Reports | Medium | Seven reports with a title and one line each; no preview, no say of which report answers which question. | Group by question ("How much did repairs cost?", "Which vehicles break most?") and show a sample row count before downloading. |
| Users (Admin) | Medium | After creating a user the screen does not say what that person must do next (log in with the emailed temporary password, change it). | A confirmation panel with those steps and a "Resend credentials" button. |
| Vehicle Categories | Low | No hint that deleting needs the category to be empty. | Disable delete with a tooltip showing the number of vehicles using it. |
| Vehicle Location | Low | Maps and hubs have no first-use guide. | One-line first-use hint with the Add Hub button. |
| Mechanic My Tasks | Medium | Cards show totals (Assigned, Pending, Submitted) but the list is not sorted by what needs doing first. | Sort rejected, then active, then waiting; highlight the next job to start. |
| Activity Log | Low | 25 rows per page with no date filter shortcuts. | "Today / This week" quick filters. |

## C. Suggested build order

1. **Quick wins (about one day):** G1 (action names), G2 (turn banner), T1 (clear Needs Repair shortcut), T2, T6, S2, S3, R1.
2. **Next:** T3 (stepper), T4 (ticket age), G3 (empty states), Admin Dashboard cards, Mechanic list order.
3. **Needs a decision first:** T5 (two repair types), R2 (undo confirm), G6 (badge meaning).
4. **Later:** Reports grouping, notice review (G4), Users confirmation panel.

## D. What I did not check

Notice text and links for each notice type, the Super Admin pages, the public pages, and the phone layout of the new Readiness pages were not walked in this audit.
